Waypoint consistency

Walk a short, repeatable path (for example, two aisle ends or two pillars). As you pass each one, tap the matching button. Do several A → B → A → B passes. The spread of all A taps, all B taps, and the A-to-B distances is your real-world precision — no tape measure needed. Reported accuracy can be optimistic, so trust this more than the big number above.

Stand-still jitter test

Stand in one place, start the test, and wait 20–30 seconds (or longer). Spread is the farthest any reading got from the average position, in feet. A large spread means the phone is wandering even while you are not moving.
